ONG NHO - KIEM TRA WEBSITE (chay tren may tinh)

1. Cai Node.js ban LTS (mot lan): https://nodejs.org
2. Giai nen thu muc nay, bam dup file  chay-app.bat
   (lan dau mat 1-2 phut de cai dat)
3. Trinh duyet tu mo http://localhost:3000. Dong cua so den de tat app.

Dien thoai cung wifi: mo http://<IP-may-tinh>:3000

LUU Y
- File .env chua khoa OpenAI cua ban. KHONG gui file nay cho nguoi khac
  va KHONG dua len GitHub.
- Muc Thong ke Google can dia chi cong khai de khach cap quyen,
  chay tren may tinh thi khach khong bam duoc.
